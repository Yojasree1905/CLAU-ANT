    Voice Commands Guide

    Speak naturally anytime using the "Hey Nav" wake phrase, or tap the mic button:

    "Hey Nav, take me to [place]" Routes to destination
    "Hey Nav, where is [place]" Finds room or amenity
    "Hey Nav, repeat" Replays last guidance
    "Hey Nav, next" Manually advance a step
    "Hey Nav, where am I" Reports current leg & distance
    "Hey Nav, switch floor" Cycles between venues
    "Hey Nav, calibrate waypoints" Opens outdoor calibration
    "Hey Nav, stop" Cancels current route
    "Hey Nav, help" Spoken list of commands
